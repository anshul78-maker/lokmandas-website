About the school

Inspiring minds, shaping futures.

Dr. Lokmandas Public School is a CBSE-affiliated, co-educational school in Etah, Uttar Pradesh, welcoming children from Nursery to Class 12. We nurture capable, compassionate learners through academic rigour, creative exploration, and a deeply rooted culture of respect.

Academic distinction

Thoughtful teaching and strong foundations that prepare every learner for what comes next.

Character & wellbeing

A safe, inclusive environment grounded in kindness, integrity, and personal responsibility.

Whole-child growth

Sport, arts, leadership, and service help students discover who they can become.
