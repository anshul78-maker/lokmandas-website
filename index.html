Thoughts from our leadership

From the Principal’s desk

Principal

Dr. Lokmandas Public School, Etah
A school is not built of bricks alone — it is built of the belief we place in every child.

Dear parents and students, every child who walks through our gates carries a light of their own. Our work is to protect that light and help it grow brighter — through curiosity, discipline and genuine care.

In our classrooms we value the question as much as the answer. We want our learners to read deeply, think clearly, speak with confidence and act with kindness. Results matter, and we are proud of them; but what matters most is the person a child becomes — honest, resilient and ready to serve.

Together with our devoted teachers and the trust of our parents, we promise to walk beside every child, every step of the way.

PrincipalWith warm regards
From the Chairman’s desk

Chairman

Dr. Lokmandas Public School, Etah
Knowledge matters — but knowledge joined with character is what truly changes the world.

Our institution was founded on a simple, enduring promise: that the children of Etah deserve an education equal to the very best. Carrying that vision forward, we have invested in bright classrooms, laboratories, sport and the arts, so that every learner can discover their strengths.

Yet buildings and results are only the beginning. We want our students to leave these gates rooted in our values and open to the world — confident in who they are, respectful of others, and determined to give back to the community that raised them.

I thank our parents for their faith and our teachers for their dedication. The future we are building together belongs to our children.

ChairmanWith best wishes
